How to play

Schness in four rules

  1. The board is 4 × 4Files a–d, ranks 1–4. White moves up the board, Black moves down.
  2. Kings go down firstWhite places their king anywhere on rank 1, then Black places theirs on rank 4. Play begins with White.
  3. Move or deployOn your turn, either move a piece on the board by normal chess rules, or take a piece from your reserve and place it on any empty square.
  4. Captures come backA piece you capture goes into your own reserve and can be deployed later, so material never leaves the game. Win by checkmate.

Two things that trip people up

A deployed piece may not put the enemy king in check on the turn it lands. And a piece dropped on the far row is not promoted; there are no pawns.
