Quick reminder

How to play Schness

Place

  • Place your king on any highlighted square of your home row.
  • Deploy reserve pieces on empty squares, but a deployment may not give check.

Play

  • Move pieces using normal chess movement.
  • If checked, move, capture, or deploy a reserve piece to block the attack.
  • Captured pieces return to their original owner’s reserve.

Finish

  • Checkmate wins.
  • Stalemate and threefold repetition are draws.
