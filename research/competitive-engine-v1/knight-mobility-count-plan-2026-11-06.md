# Count legal knight destinations as a bitset

Checkpoint: PR #123 a1e2a3694aee7f874a1f64071277616474aadc35. The current count-only generator visits each knight destination and applies the same four existing filters separately. The proposed exact shortcut intersects the precomputed knight destinations with the current evasion mask and any absolute pin mask, removes friendly squares and the opposing king, then counts the remaining bits. It changes neither the legal action list nor any evaluation weight. The full generator remains in use when the count-only preconditions do not hold, including missing/multiple mover kings or unrecognized phase. No new dependency or cache.
