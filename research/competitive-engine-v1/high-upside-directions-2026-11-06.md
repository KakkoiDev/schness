# What could move Schness playing strength at 100 ms?

The original JavaScript baseline won 2/32 against pinned Fairy-Stockfish. The history-only screen won one additional game; several other isolated candidates tied or fell below the baseline. Capture-only quiescence moved median depth from six to seven and still won 2/32. Recent exact speed experiments did not meet their fixed-depth and equal-time gates. Earlier bounded analysis found four late choices that were optimal under the diagnostic at the played depth but worse than alternatives at depth six; three were already badly behind. These are finite-horizon observations, not proof of the cause of the win-rate gap. The 64-position four-term ridge attempt worsened both training and grouped validation error and stays closed.

## First choice: a learned prior for ordinary interior moves

Keep alpha-beta, the exact legal move generator, score evaluation, and root tie policy. Test one inexpensive action ranker at ordinary interior nodes, initially in nonselective search. Use board and reserve context and the action itself, so the prior can distinguish otherwise similar deployments, retreat squares, and capture consequences. Unlike the failed previous-action reply rule, the signal would come from fresh independent action preferences and condition on the current position. No move pruning. Static move labels and centipawn residuals from the rejected dataset are excluded.

First measure whether there is room for this: on fresh grouped, outcome-inclusive paths, record where the unchanged ordering places the best move at a common, completed reference horizon. Keep wins, losses, draws, checks, and incomplete labels, and report those strata separately. Distinguish genuine ordering room from the reference search disagreeing with itself across adjacent horizons. Before any queries, freeze the positions, grouping, completion rules, caps and stop criteria. Keep benchmark and confirmation starts out of training and validation. Only if there is broad room for improvement, train one small ranker on disjoint positions and demand improvement over current ordering on grouped held-out top-three recall and nonselective node count. Then require net search benefit at equal 100 ms, with the model cost included, and independent tactical parity. With LMR on, reordering can alter reductions and decisions; the nonselective equivalence check cannot establish selective equivalence. No teacher call at runtime.

## Second choice: a compact board-and-reserve value model

A model with board-piece-square and reserve interactions could represent situations the four existing scalar terms cannot distinguish. This means a fresh data family and an explicitly different target: game outcomes from diverse independent games, or independently obtained search preferences kept separate from outcome labels. Group full game/start families before fitting; reserve a fresh test set and compare both decision quality and JavaScript inference cost before integrating with search. Fairy-Stockfish's variant NNUE trainer establishes a practical precedent, not compatibility with Schness's exact rules or a forecast of strength. A small incremental model is more plausible at 100 ms than invoking a large network per node. If neither fresh labels nor cheap inference can be demonstrated, stop rather than reuse the failed fit.

## Lower priority: a new search architecture

Policy/value-guided tree search has research precedent in chess, shogi and crazyhouse. It also changes several components at once and needs a fast, good policy and value model before a fair 100 ms test. Reconsider only after the simpler ranker or value model demonstrates a usable signal. Keep Rust paused. Keep the pinned opponent and all screen conditions unchanged. Only a qualified candidate reaches the 17/32 screen gate, then the frozen 98/192 confirmation gate; the 51% raw objective remains unmet.

Their results concern other engines and conditions. This memo is research prioritization; no new match, queries, or training ran.
