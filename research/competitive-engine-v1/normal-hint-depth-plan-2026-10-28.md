# Preregistered normal-search hint overwrite audit

Current normal search stores `actionIdentity(best.line.action)` under the history-independent board hint key after every completed node, without the searched depth. A later shallow visit can overwrite a deeper visit's chosen action for the same board. That is legal ordering information, not a score-cache identity bug. The already rejected q-node hint experiment is separate: this question concerns the existing normal-search hint map, with no q hint addition.

Falsifiable question: in a fixed bounded workload, do shallower completed normal nodes actually replace deeper normal hints with a different action, and are these replaced hints subsequently consumed? If there are no meaningful differing overwrites and later uses, stop before a candidate. Occurrence alone does not show that the deeper hint is better, or establish a strength gain.

Instrument only the existing normal hint map. Maintain a diagnostic shadow map recording last stored depth/action and previous deeper overwritten action, but do not affect ordering, score reuse, pruning or table eviction. Match shadow eviction to the existing bounded FIFO map. Count updates, shallow-over-deep updates, differing-action shallow-over-deep updates, and later ordering reads of those overwritten entries. Retain the first12 differing overwrite positions/actions/depths in deterministic traversal order, without score or outcome selection. Record incomplete queries and any mismatch.

Use the existing160 outcome-inclusive/random snapshots in js-legal-recaptures.json in their existing order. Exactly one depth5 nonreduced pass, q4checks/evasion2/cacheEvaluationtrue/cacheQuiescencefalse,100000nodes/200ms per query. All experimental flags false, no RNG. Compare score/action/nodes/completed depth/PV to an uninstrumented current-source pass under the same caps; do not assert equality when timing interrupts at different nodes. For jointly completed depth5 queries, require exact score/action/node agreement. Assert legal PV/input restoration throughout. No time or node cap increases, reruns for favorable results, match or candidate is authorized by this measurement alone.

If later work is justified, a depth-preferred ordering policy needs a separate bounded default-preserving prototype, independent exact tests and cost/decision evidence. History-sensitive bound reuse remains unchanged. Do not resurrect q-cache/hints, history alias keys, root shuffle, category ordering, or the exhausted three-piece/compulsory-Q families.
