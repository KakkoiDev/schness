# Ordered child mask reuse

Checkpoint: PR #123 9cb779abf424b167afe0b207d349b351a2329461. Competitive move scoring computes nine child board masks, then a reversible transition computes the same masks again when searching that action. This trial changes only their lifetime. It does not change legality, scoring, ordering, history keys, or the benchmark.

Freeze a control and one temporary candidate. In the competitive reversible adapter, retain the masks computed by ordering in a per-ply slab, with action-object identity indexing for that order call. Copy the nine masks into the existing child scratch on an identity hit, otherwise compute them as before. A new child entry invalidates its old frame before any child ordering; returning from it leaves the parent frame intact. A new order call replaces the frame index. Root reorders can only reuse the original root action objects and the unchanged parent board. Never store masks in transposition, hint or public position data. Reference, immutable, and unordered paths must fall back. Do not change source outside the frozen research copy.

Before timing, require one independent verification of every reused mask against recomputation on the diagnostic roots plus all 36 variants, including legal PV replay, exact caller restoration, and exact paired result/counter parity for completed fixed-depth roots. Include setup, capture, drop, repetition, and stopped-search coverage through the portable contract and targeted parity. Timing uses a separately frozen candidate without recomputation assertions. Generate 32 independent legal paths with sorted action keys, LCG 1664525/1013904223 modulo 2^32, seed 20263611, targets 8/16/24/32 repeated. Retain terminal and incomplete paths without replacement.

Measure serial ABBA (control/candidate/candidate/control) on all 32 paths at depth 4, 100,000 nodes and 200 ms, q4 checks/evasion 2, LMR off, evaluation cache on, q cache off, other experimental flags off. Require all fixed-depth pairs complete or terminal and match action, score, variations, PV, depth and nodes. Separately run the unchanged 100 ms search options in the same ABBA arrangement and report completed depths and actual times; timed outputs can vary with the amount of work finished. Require at least 5% lower aggregate fixed-depth elapsed and no drop in mean 100 ms completed depth. Report actual reuse/fallback counts separately from timing; timing instrumentation may not count mask operations in the control alone. Stop after this one trial if any gate fails. No matches or production adoption follow automatically.
