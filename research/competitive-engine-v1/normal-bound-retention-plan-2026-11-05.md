# Normal search: audit overwritten complementary bounds

Normal alpha-beta stores one exact value, lower bound, or upper bound per full history-sensitive key and remaining depth. A later bound can replace one of the opposite type. Could retaining that complementary bound have closed a later search window that the real cache left open?

Audit only normal-node lookups and writes. A shadow entry follows the real cache's key lifetime and FIFO eviction. Keep the strongest seen upper and lower bounds until eviction or an exact store. Count opposite-type replacements, later lookups where the real entry leaves the window open but applying the shadow bounds would close it, and inverted shadow intervals. Exclude inverted intervals from opportunities and report them separately. Count at most one opportunity per lookup; retain the first 12 examples per arm, including key, depth, original window and bounds. Do not modify the real table, bounds, move order, or reported decisions. No q-cache changes.

Take indices 0, 4, …, 124 from the first 128 cases of js-legal-recaptures.json in stored order, with no replacements. Run one instrumented pass and one uninstrumented pass per arm, using the same frozen JS source and full position histories. Use competitive q4 with checks, evasion 2, evaluation cache on, q cache off, no RNG, other experimental flags off. First arm: iterative depth 4, LMR off, 100,000 nodes and 200 ms per root. Second arm: request 20, LMR on, 100 ms and the unchanged production node setting. Keep all capped results. Require identical action, score, depth, nodes and PV on jointly completed depth-4 queries. Replay PVs and check input restoration on every query. Timed-arm differences are not attribution or strength evidence; shadow overhead is not a speed comparison.

Stop before a prototype unless both arms show at least 32 potential cutoffs across at least eight different roots, the exact arm has no inverted intervals, and parity/restoration pass. Selective results are telemetry only; complementary selective bounds need not describe one common exact value. Even a pass permits only a separately planned correctness and cost prototype. No opponent query, game screen, production change, or promotion. Archive the plan, exact input/source, scripts, outputs and logs and verify published bytes.
