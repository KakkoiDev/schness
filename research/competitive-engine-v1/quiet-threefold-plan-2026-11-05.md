# Quiet moves that immediately draw at the quiescence horizon

At a nonchecked positive-budget quiescence node, the engine searches captures and checks. It can omit a quiet legal move whose successor is already a threefold draw. Unlike a generic quiet defense, this action has a certified value of zero under the actual game rules. If the moving side is losing according to the returned quiescence value, an available immediate draw contradicts that value as a game-theoretic bound; it does not establish a lost root win or a strength result.

Audit only nodes that reach the existing noisy-action filter. Do not alter choices, score, counters, move order, keys, stand-pat cutoffs, zero-budget leaves, or checked evasions. At nodes with a prior key at count two or greater, use the existing legal list minus the existing noisy list. Verify prospective moves by public transition and draw adjudication on the exact position with its original repetition counts. Count nodes with a quiet immediate threefold, and completed audited nodes whose returned score has the wrong sign for the mover given that draw option (negative at a maximizing node, positive at a minimizing node). Report these signs as search observations, since narrow windows may return bounds. Retain the first 12 such contradictions in traversal order with full history, action, window, value and PV. If a node is interrupted, do not classify its return. Use at most 1,000 auxiliary successor transitions per root; retain and label any skipped work when the auxiliary cap is reached.

Use the first 128 existing outcome-inclusive snapshots from js-legal-recaptures.json, exactly once per arm, no filtering or replacements. Compare instrumented and uninstrumented competitive searches at iterative depth 4, q4 with checks, evasion 2, LMR off, evaluation cache on, q cache off, other experimental flags off, no RNG, 100,000 nodes and 200 ms per root. Retain every incomplete query. Require legal root decisions and PVs and restored input throughout; require identical score, action, PV, depth and nodes for jointly completed queries and at least 96 jointly complete. Instrumented times are not speed measurements.

Stop without a prototype unless there are at least 16 completed contradictory nodes across at least eight different roots and all correctness gates pass. A pass warrants a separate correctness and cost plan only, with full-history independent replay and no blanket admission of quiet moves. No repeat probing with expanded horizons, search policy changes, opponent queries, matches, or production changes. Archive the plan, invoked runtime modules, exact inputs, script, all outputs, and logs.
