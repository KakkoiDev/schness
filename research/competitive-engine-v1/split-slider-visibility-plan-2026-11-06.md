# Split occupancy slider visibility preregistration

Question: can two small visibility lookups replace the per-attacker blocker loop in the boolean board-mask attack predicate and improve 100 ms search throughput? The control is the frozen unchanged runtime from the knight mobility experiment; the candidate replaces only the slider portion of masksAttack. This is neither a mobility count change nor reuse of child masks. It changes no action ranking, budget, cache, rule, or evaluation.

For each target, precompute the aligned slider squares whose strictly intermediate squares are clear, separately for the low and high eight occupancy bits. A slider is visible exactly when it survives both halves. Keep the leaper checks and empty slider early exit; leave attacker enumeration and drop-specific code untouched. Test every target and all 65,536 occupancies against an independent geometric ray walk, then check the candidate with the portable contract (all 36 variants), independent mate tests and fixed-depth parity.

Use 32 fresh quiet shared six-slot capture-bank roots from deterministic uniformly sampled legal paths (seed 20264037, cycling 8/16/24/32 plies; retain terminals); no archived reversals. Two control and two candidate passes in ABBA order at depth four, LMR off, 100,000 nodes and 200 ms as failure caps; compare complete fixed-depth results, PV legality and restoration. Then use ABBA at the unchanged 100 ms benchmark settings. Require exact preflight and fixed-depth parity, at least 5% aggregate fixed-depth wall-time saving and nondecreasing mean completed depth at 100 ms. Any miss closes the prototype without tuning or reruns. Passing permits considering the separately specified competitive screen; timing is not strength. Keep frozen sources, inputs, raw results and logs; no production edits.
