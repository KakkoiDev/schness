The library, for players

Players lead and the research archive moves to a second tab, as the user decided on 8 November (recorded in DECISIONS.md). The nine cards are the shipped markup, captured from the live library with the result filter and “Shortest first” applied, then given player copy.

The shipped library, measured against its own data

It opens on sixteen draws
data/games.json holds 462 threefold draws, 303 White wins, 270 Black wins and 64 ply-limit draws. “As recorded” is the default sort, and the first sixteen records are all threefold draws: the first decisive game is card 17. A laptop shows six cards on load, a phone one.
Its first sentence is for a researcher
“AI archive”, then “981 repeated records collapsed by exact move sequence”, then 2,080 original records and 78.3 average plies, then four filters, one of which picks a Sharp engine version. All true, and all before a single game.
Every card says it twice
“Sharp v1 vs Steady v1 · Sharp v2 vs Steady v1”: one game recorded under two engine versions, listed as two matchups, followed by “13 plies · 7 moves · seen 4×” and a record number.

What this screen changes

  • Watch opens on wins, shortest first. 573 decisive games, 86 of them in 30 plies or fewer; the shortest is seven moves. The default view is the one worth replaying.
  • One question, not four. Who wins: either, White or Black. The result and sort controls that already exist do the work, plus one value the result filter does not have yet: “either side won”.
  • A card is a result, a length and a matchup. “White won in 7 moves”, “Sharp vs Steady”. Engine versions, record numbers and repeat counts move to Archive, where they mean something.
  • Archive keeps everything: the four statistics, all four filters, every sort and every record, unchanged, behind its tab.

Engineering prerequisites for deliverable 6

  • A decisive value for the result filter. matchesFilters compares one code; “either side won” is w or b.
  • Watch defaults to decisive and shortest first; Archive keeps “as recorded”.
  • Player copy for the card, “White won in 7 moves” and a version-free matchup, in EN and JA, with the existing strings kept for Archive.
