A stale entry DECISIONS.md
“puzzles.html joins the rest of the design: its board no longer sits under the fixed action bar.” At rest it does, at every phone height measured. What is true is that the page reserves room below the content, so the board can be scrolled clear of it.
Why not cap the board respected
styles.css records the rejected alternative: capping the board at calc(100svh - 32rem) took it to 188px at 390×700 and 128px at 360×640, and the page scrolled anyway. That reasoning holds. The fix has to come out of what is above the board, not out of the board.

What this screen changes

  • The puzzle mix closes into one row. On a phone the shipped page forces the settings open and hides their summary: four level toggles and two switches, every visit, above the board. Here it is the shipped <details>, closed, with its summary saying what is set — “Mate 1–4”.
  • One heading on a phone, not two. “Find the checkmate” above “White to move · mate in 2” says it twice; the prompt is the visible heading and the h1 stays for the outline and screen readers, visually hidden.
  • The resting feedback strip hides until there is something to say, as the rules stepper’s already does: at rest it repeats the prompt, and it sat half under the bar.
  • Nothing else moves. The board keeps the full width, the bar stays fixed, the room reserved below the content stays. The board starts at 281 instead of 413.
