The variant picker

One select holds 37 options today: 36 presets and Random, in source order, each one a bare label. This is that list opened out — grouped by what a preset actually changes, with the engine’s own sentence on the one you have chosen. Every group below was derived from VARIANTS, and the build refuses to emit the page unless all 36 appear exactly once.

The grouping, and why it is a list and not a grid

Four groups, Classic 1 · Captures 10 · Checking drops 19 · Setup 6, summing to 36.

A two-axis grid — capture policy against drop legality — would be tidier, and it would lie. The presets are not a cross product: capture policy and setup each come in quiet and checking pairs, but the eighteen drop restrictions are fixed at allowCheckingDrops: true and combine with nothing. There is no “Capture bank 4 · knight checks only”, and a grid would draw an empty cell where a player would expect one. A grouped list says what exists.

It cross-checks against the engine split
Grouped by what a player chooses: 1 Classic, 10 captures, 19 checking drops, 6 setup. Grouped by what the refusal guard does: 1 Classic, 8 quiet experiments, 9 that never refuse, 18 that need a reason of their own. Both reach 36, and no preset is in two places.
It needs almost no new copy
The rows are the shipped VARIANT_LABELS and the sentence is the shipped description(). Only the four group names and six sub-headings are new — ten strings to translate, not thirty-six.
Every row carries one of three tags
quiet (9), checks (9), limited (18). Derived from allowCheckingDrops and whether the preset carries a restricting experiment, and it is the same split the refusal guard makes. It is also the one fact that changes how a turn feels, and in the current list it is buried mid-label or absent — “Three-turn grace” does not say that it ever allows a check.
A defect in these reference screens fixed here
Every design screen since October has carried game-page on <body>. The app puts it on <main class="game game-page"> and nowhere else. On a body it caps the page at 66rem and drags in the match layout’s grid placements, which is what hid the arena’s trays above its board and what was hiding every tray label on the capture-bank screen below 760px. The three November screens drop it; the five from October still carry it and need the same change with a re-measure.

Three things the copy audit found for deliverable 6

  • A lower-case side, mid-sentence. All four full-setup presets render “black takes the first normal turn.” from ${rules.firstPlayer}, because the value is the internal token. Four strings.
  • Descriptions run to 201 characters. The longest is Random hidden armies; the four full-setup strings are 191–193. They are built for a research log, not a row in a picker, which is why this screen shows one at a time rather than all 36.
  • A research disclaimer is shown to players. Both hidden-army presets end “Uniform legal arrangement sampling, not a hidden-information AI.” That belongs in the library, not under a button someone is about to press.
