The home page stays what DECISIONS.md says it is: a board, not a picture of one, playing Classic against the same bot worker as every other surface. What changes is where the doors are, and how many.

The shipped home, measured

No door above the fold on a laptop
At 1280×800 the first card starts at y = 884. The board is visible; every way off the page is not.
Five cards, and the primary is second
Random variant was added above Bot arena, as its own bordered card. The page now offers an experimental ruleset before the default one, and has two doors to the same arena with two different promises. DECISIONS.md describes four.
Five arrows at five positions
.mode never declares its columns, so the arrow column is sized by the text beside it: 118, 78, 204, 195 and 189px wide at 1280. The arrows wander down the stack.
The header wraps at 1280
.lobby-page .game caps the page at 42rem, so on the widest screens the brand, the four destinations and Rules / Dark / 日本語 do not fit one row.
A stale entry DECISIONS.md
“At phone width the board is display: none, on purpose.” It has not been since 954ceff removed .lobby-play{display:none}; a phone gets a 272px board. The behaviour is the better one — it makes “take this position further” true on the device where that card is the only door — but the entry needs rewriting in the commit that ships this.

What this screen changes

  • Four doors again. Random variant folds into the arena card, whose line becomes “stronger bots, rule variants, replay”. Random stays one tap away, as the first row of the arena’s picker. One changed string, in two languages.
  • Board and doors side by side from 900px. The page widens to the 66rem every other page uses, and every card sits beside the board, above an 800px fold.
  • Arrows in one column. minmax(0, 1fr) auto on the card, so the arrow is always at the card’s edge.
  • The reserves are named on a phone. The same styles.css:1534 gap as the arena: .lobby-seat has no player row, so its labels come back.
  • Unchanged: Classic only, no setup, no strength control, no variant chip. The home page starts a game; the arena is where you change it.

Proposed. Header, footer, board, reserves and cards are the shipped markup and classes, copied from index.html at build time. The two-column layout, the card columns and the restored labels are new and marked PROPOSED in design.css.
