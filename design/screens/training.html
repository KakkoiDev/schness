What this screen changes

  • The hint lives in the turn card. One line under “White to move”, tagged Training so it never reads as the game speaking. Its four states keep the shipped wording and data-state: opportunity, danger, caution, clear.
  • The score stays with its switch. “White has mate · estimate” sits under the toggles; the bar’s aria-valuetext carries the same words, so nothing is said twice on screen.
  • The bar takes its width from the board, never from a file. When it is on, the board gives up the bar’s width plus a gap and the bar stands beside it at every size.
  • Strength is the chip. “Level 3 · Steady”, and tapping it opens the two sliders below.

The bot chip, opened

Level 0–7 with the five named stops, and a think-time cap. Default level 3 and 1 s, as decided. The think-time range is proposed: ¼ s to 8 s on a doubling scale, because level 7’s worst measured move was 8.15 s and a linear slider would spend most of its travel on waits nobody chooses.
