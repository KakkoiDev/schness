What this screen changes

  • One bar instead of a heading and a form. Variant, seat and opponent become chips that read as sentences and open pickers. The headline goes; the nav already carries it. On a phone only the variant chip stays, and the rest moves behind one button — partly for room, and partly because a wrapping bar changes the height the board is sized from.
  • The board is bounded by the window. min(37rem, the column, the height left over), so the turn line and both trays are on screen with it. Measured: the lower tray ends at 736 of 800 on desktop, 611 of 812 at 390, and 577 of 640 on a short phone, where the board is still 324px.
  • Both banks carry a count. Yours reads 3 / 4, Black’s 1 / 4. Theirs matters as much as yours: a full opponent bank means the piece they take from you comes straight back to your side.
  • The tray keeps its label on a phone. styles.css:1534 visually hides .reserve-label below 760px, because the shipped match layout names each side in its own player row. The arena has no such row, so without this the two trays are told apart by position and piece colour alone — and colour is never the only signal.
  • Refusals are drawn, not narrated. The five crossed squares are exactly the d‑file and the first rank through Black’s king: the shape of the rule rather than a list of exceptions.
  • Training tools start open, in the rail beside the board rather than 627px below it.

A suggestion that measured badly rejected

A 2×2 block for a capacity-4 bank on phones reads well in isolation, and it was built and measured. It costs 44px of height in each of the two trays, and the board is sized from what is left: on a 640px-tall phone it fell from 324px to 184px, about 46px a square.

So the rule is now one row, wrapping only when a row will not fit. Six tiles and their gaps are 330px and a 360px phone leaves 328, so capacity 6 wraps to 3+3 below 375px. Capacities 3, 4 and 5 are 162, 218 and 274px and fit every phone, so they never wrap. One principle, and the tray’s shape still carries the capacity.

Decided: level 0–7 plus a think-time cap 8 Nov
Two sliders replace the named levels; the Steady chip above is superseded. Level is search depth: 0–3 answer in under 3 ms, 4 in about 5, 5 in 25, 6 in 83 (worst 420), and 7 in a median 1.3 s with a worst case of 8.2 s, which is why the range stops at 7. Think time caps every move so no level can stall the game. Marked stops: Learning 2, Steady 3 (default), Sharp 4, Strong 6, Max 7; default cap 1 s. Speeds from one Mac, not a phone, and not a skill measure. Not yet in watch-ui.js: an engineering prerequisite.
Decided: advantage bar off by default 8 Nov
The user chose off. The bar stays one tap away in Training tools, beside the board; on by default would make every game a graded one.
