The hidden-army reveal

Random hidden armies, quiet drops, seed 20261108: White’s king on b1, Black’s on a4, then both home ranks filled by hiddenSetupPlacements. The position above was reproduced from the engine and matches what the shipped arena draws for the same seed.

What the shipped arena does with it

The reveal has no moment
One click places White’s king, and the next frame holds eight pieces. The status goes straight from “place your king” to “White to move · Your seat”. Nothing says the armies were drawn, or that yours was not your choice.
The transcript invents three moves
It reads 2 @Na1 @Bb4  3 @Bc1 @Rc4  4 @Rd1 @Nd4: six drops, paired and numbered as though White chose a knight on a1 and Black answered with a bishop on b4. DECISIONS.md: the six-drop serialization “is for replay, never a sequence of informed setup choices”. History already carries simultaneousReveal: true on each of them; nothing reads it.
Replay walks through positions that never existed
Stepping back from live shows “Reviewing ply 5 of 8” with two white pieces down and one black — six intermediate boards no one ever faced. Branch is correctly disabled there; Edit this position is not.

What this screen changes

  • The turn card names the event. “Armies revealed”, and one sentence on what that means. It stays until White’s first move.
  • The six revealed squares are marked for the same turn, with a corner notch and “revealed” in each square’s name. The kings are not marked: they were placed, not drawn.
  • The transcript has one reveal row, both armies side by side, unnumbered and written as positions rather than as @ drops, because nobody dropped them. Moves resume at 2. The row is the honest unit: two simultaneous draws, not six alternating choices.
  • Replay steps over it. Back from here goes to kings-only, forward from there goes to the full board. With no half-revealed position to land on, “a replay cannot branch halfway through a hidden reveal” stops being a rule enforced by a disabled button and becomes a position that does not exist. Edit follows for free.
  • Both reserves are empty and drawn that way: three dashed slots each, Classic capture policy, so every piece that is taken comes back to its owner.

Engineering prerequisites for deliverable 6

  • The transcript reads simultaneousReveal. The flag exists on all six history entries; the renderer groups them into one row.
  • The transport treats the group as one ply. Review indices skip from before the first reveal entry to after the last.
  • A one-turn “revealed” state for squares, cleared on the next action, plus the eyebrow and status strings in EN and JA.
