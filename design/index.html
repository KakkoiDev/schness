What is proposed, and what already ships

Everything here uses the shipped classes except the additions marked PROPOSED in design.css: the brand mark as an image rather than the Arial letterform tile, and the capture-bank tray's capacity count, at-capacity state and permanently-lost slot. .square.refused was proposed in October and has since shipped into styles.css, so its marker is gone. The rest is the system as it stands.
