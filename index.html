How to play Schness

1. Your army

  • The board is a 4 × 4 grid.
  • Each player owns one king, one rook, one bishop, and one knight.
  • The board begins completely empty.

2. Place the kings

  • White places their king first, anywhere on White’s home row.
  • Black then places their king anywhere on Black’s home row.

3. Take a turn

  • Either move one of your pieces already on the board, using normal chess movement, or deploy one piece from your reserve.
  • A reserve piece may be deployed on any empty square.
  • A deployment may never immediately check the opposing king.
  • You must always answer check and may never leave your own king in check.

4. Capture and reuse

  • Capture pieces as in chess.
  • A captured piece returns to its original owner’s reserve.
  • Its owner may deploy it again on a later turn.

5. End the game

  • Checkmate wins the game.
  • Stalemate is a draw.
  • Repeating the same position three times is a draw.
